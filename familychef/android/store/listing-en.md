## Full description (max 4000 characters)

FamilyChef plans a week of meals for your whole family within your budget and builds a shopping list priced in your local currency.

How it works:
• Enter your weekly budget and how many adults and children you are feeding.
• Choose your diet: vegetarian, vegan, no pork, gluten-free, dairy-free and more.
• Exclude foods you don't eat or are allergic to.
• Add what you already have at home — FamilyChef uses it first.

You get:
• A 7-day menu: breakfast, lunch and dinner, varied from day to day.
• Recipes with exact amounts for your family size.
• A shopping list grouped by store section, with the number of packs and the cost.
• A clear total: what you will spend, what is left of your budget and the cost per person per day.

Real local prices, not converted dollars:
• Local prices and pack sizes for 12 countries — the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, Germany, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Azerbaijan, Russia and Belarus — each in its own currency.
• City-level adjustments for big cities like New York, London, Toronto, Sydney and Munich.
• Fruit and vegetable prices follow the seasons — including the southern hemisphere.
• Different price in your store? Edit it in the list and the menu is recalculated around your prices.
• Familiar dishes for each country, from cottage pie, jacket potatoes and spaghetti bolognese to chili, plov and borscht.

Handy in the store:
• Tick off items as you shop.
• Share the shopping list to any messenger with one tap.
• Tap "Another menu" for more variety.

No ads. Your settings stay on your phone.

Prices are estimates and are updated regularly. Recipes are not medical advice.
